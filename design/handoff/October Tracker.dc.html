TURN 1 · TODAY SCREEN

Three ways to see both of you

Coral is you, teal is her. Everything else is mono. Drag across a ruler tile (sleep, outside, steps) to log, drag up and down on water or reading, and tap the pills. The glow shows only while you drag.

1aSplit tiles

One screen. Your value is the big number, hers sits under it in teal. Nothing to switch.

1bMe · Her · Both toggle

Same tiles with a switch on top. "Both" matches 1a, "Me" hides her for focus, "Her" is view-only.

1cSwipe between days

Your day and her day are two pages side by side. Swipe or tap You / Her. Each page stays clean.
