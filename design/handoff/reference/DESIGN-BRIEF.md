# App context — Project October tracker

> Context only, for a fresh Claude Design brainstorm. No design decisions included. Paste everything below the line.

---

## What it is
An app where **my partner and I** track the same set of body and mind goals every day for **October 2026 (1–31 Oct)**. Each of us logs our own day and can see the other's log. It's built for one month and has a finish line.

## Who and when
- **Two people, each on their own phone.** It's private to just the two of us.
- **Used twice a day:** quick logging as things happen (after the gym, after water) plus a close-out at night (gratitude, pages read).
- I'm a designer with a neurodivergent brain. My past plans faded after the first push, so this has to still work on low-energy days.

## How it should feel between us
A **quiet mirror**. No score and no competition. We each simply see how the other's day is going.

## The goals
**Body**
1. **Water 2.5 L a day.** Water only. No soft drinks, juices or packaged drinks. Black coffee with no sugar is allowed.
2. **No junk.** Each person gets **3 cheat meals for the whole month**. Junk = fried/oily food, packaged/instant food, the pani puri family, baked items, and sugar items (chocolates, sweets).
3. **Sleep 7–8 hours.**
4. **Gym 4 times a week,** with a run in **at least 3** of those sessions.
5. **10,000 steps a day.**
6. **20 minutes outside a day.**

**Mind**
7. **No phone during meals.**
8. **Gratitude:** write 3 things each night.
9. **Read 10 pages a day,** or 50 pages a week.

Some goals are daily, some are weekly (gym, runs, and reading as a fallback), and one is monthly (cheat meals).

## Technical constraints
- A **progressive web app**: installable on the phone, no app store.
- Needs to sync between our two phones and work offline.
- Planned stack: vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, Firebase (Google sign-in, only our 2 accounts), hosted on GitHub Pages.
- Needs to be ready by 1 October.

## What I want from this session
Brainstorm the design from scratch: structure, how logging works, how we see each other, and the visual direction.
